# Comment Templates, Phrasing & Anchor Text Best Practices

## 1. Contextual Relevance Principles
To maximize SEO value and ensure long-term approval from blog moderators:
1. **Never copy-paste identical comments.** Each blog post addresses unique nuances (e.g., regional foundation types, exterior waterproofing methods, French drains, sump pump failures).
2. **Cite specific points from the article.** Demonstrate you actually read the content.
3. **Keep the tone helpful and professional.** Write from the perspective of an experienced local specialist or educated property owner.

---

## 2. Contextual Anchor Text Architecture
Anchor text must blend naturally into the sentence and emphasize topic-relevant services rather than raw spammy brand names alone.

### Required HTML Format:
```html
<b><a href="https://yourwebsite.com/">Contextually Relevant Anchor Text</a></b>
```
